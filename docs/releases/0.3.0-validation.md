## Native delivery runtime sample

An explicitly authorized disposable Codex 0.154.0 conversation on macOS arm64
was launched with normal native permissions after the owner reviewed the exact
Flere hook definitions. A notice emitted at a real ordinary shell-tool boundary
was handled by the recipient and explicitly acknowledged. A separate idle notice
was accepted by the native queue, read by the recipient and acknowledged.

DND deferred a saved notice. After DND expired, an unsent draft continued to
prevent delivery and remained unchanged. Removing only that test draft without
Enter allowed normal idle delivery and explicit acknowledgement. The native
permissions menu also deferred a notice without changing its content or selecting
a permission. After the menu closed, its cursor layout was outside the conservative
idle guard, so that notice remained saved for an explicit inbox read on the next
user turn. No fallback keystrokes were injected. These checks do not establish
physical Windows attachment behavior
or every harness's tool-approval dialog; exact native receipts are retained
privately rather than included in this repository.
